Coconut Honey Face Pack

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon yogurt

How to Make

  1. Add coconut oil and honey into a clean bowl.
  2. Mix the ingredients well until a smooth mixture is formed.
  3. Add yogurt and mix again gently.
  4. Apply the mixture evenly on the face.
  5. Leave it for about 10–15 minutes.
  6. Wash with lukewarm water and gently pat the skin dry.

Benefits

Coconut oil and honey are natural ingredients widely used in skincare because of their moisturizing and healing properties. Coconut oil helps to nourish the skin, improve skin hydration, and protect the skin barrier with its natural fatty acids. Honey acts as a natural antibacterial and humectant ingredient that attracts moisture to the skin and helps keep it soft and smooth. The combination of coconut oil and honey helps to deeply moisturize the skin, improve skin texture, and support a healthy natural glow.
