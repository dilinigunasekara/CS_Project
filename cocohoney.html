Coconut Honey Face Pack

A simple natural face pack made with coconut oil and honey that helps moisturize the skin, improve skin texture, and support a healthy natural glow.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon yogurt

How to Make

  1. Add coconut oil and honey into a clean bowl.
  2. Mix the ingredients until a smooth mixture forms.
  3. Add yogurt and mix gently.
  4. Apply the mixture evenly on the face.
  5. Leave it for about 10–15 minutes.
  6. Wash with lukewarm water and gently pat the skin dry.

Main Ingredient Benefits

Coconut oil and honey are natural ingredients widely used in skincare because of their moisturizing and healing properties. Coconut oil helps nourish the skin, improve hydration, and protect the skin barrier with natural fatty acids. Honey acts as a natural antibacterial and humectant ingredient that attracts moisture to the skin and helps keep it soft and smooth. The combination of coconut oil and honey helps deeply moisturize the skin, improve skin texture, and support a healthy natural glow.
