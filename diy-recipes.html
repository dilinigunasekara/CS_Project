DIY Beauty Recipes

Create natural beauty products at home with simple, effective recipes

Honey & Oat Face Mask

⏱ 15 minutes ⭐ Easy 💧 For Dry & Sensitive Skin

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons raw honey
  • 1 tablespoon ground oats
  • 1 teaspoon coconut oil
  • 1 teaspoon aloe vera gel
  • Few drops of lavender essential oil (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Mix the ground oats and honey in a small bowl until well combined.
  2. Add coconut oil and aloe vera gel, stirring until smooth.
  3. Add a few drops of lavender oil if desired for extra soothing benefits.
  4. Apply the mask to clean, dry skin, avoiding the eye area.
  5. Leave on for 15-20 minutes, then rinse with warm water.
  6. Pat dry and apply your favorite moisturizer.

Tip: Use this mask 2-3 times per week for best results. Store any leftover mixture in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

Coffee Body Scrub

⏱ 10 minutes ⭐ Easy ☕ For All Skin Types

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup ground coffee (used or fresh)
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup coconut oil (melted)
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Combine ground coffee and brown sugar in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add melted coconut oil and mix until well combined.
  3. Stir in vanilla extract and cinnamon if using.
  4. Store in an airtight container at room temperature.
  5. In the shower, apply the scrub to wet skin in circular motions.
  6. Focus on areas like elbows, knees, and feet for extra exfoliation.
  7. Rinse thoroughly with warm water.

Tip: Coffee grounds help reduce cellulite appearance and improve circulation. Use 2-3 times per week for smooth, glowing skin.

Avocado Hair Mask

⏱ 20 minutes ⭐ Easy 💚 For Dry & Damaged Hair

Ingredients:

  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 tablespoons coconut oil
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 egg yolk (optional, for extra protein)

Instructions:

  1. Mash the avocado in a bowl until smooth and creamy.
  2. Add coconut oil, olive oil, and honey, mixing well.
  3. If using, add the egg yolk and mix until fully incorporated.
  4. Apply the mask to damp hair, starting from the roots to the ends.
  5. Cover your hair with a shower cap or warm towel.
  6. Leave on for 30-45 minutes for deep conditioning.
  7. Rinse thoroughly with cool water and shampoo as usual.

Tip: This mask is perfect for restoring moisture to dry, damaged hair. Use once a week for best results.

Green Tea Face Toner

⏱ 5 minutes ⭐ Very Easy 🌿 For Oily & Acne-Prone Skin

Ingredients:

  • 2 green tea bags or 2 tablespoons loose green tea
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 1 tablespoon witch hazel (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar (optional)
  • Few drops of tea tree oil (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Steep green tea in boiling water for 5-10 minutes.
  2. Allow the tea to cool completely to room temperature.
  3. Remove tea bags or strain if using loose tea.
  4. Add witch hazel, apple cider vinegar, and tea tree oil if desired.
  5. Pour into a clean spray bottle or container.
  6. Store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
  7. Apply to clean skin using a cotton pad or spray directly.

Tip: Green tea is rich in antioxidants and helps reduce inflammation. Use morning and evening after cleansing for best results.

Turmeric & Yogurt Brightening Mask

⏱ 15 minutes ⭐ Easy ✨ For Dull & Uneven Skin Tone

Ingredients:

  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 tablespoons plain yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon gram flour (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Mix turmeric powder with yogurt in a small bowl.
  2. Add honey and lemon juice, stirring until smooth.
  3. Add gram flour if using for extra exfoliation.
  4. Apply evenly to clean face and neck, avoiding the eye area.
  5. Leave on for 15-20 minutes until it dries slightly.
  6. Rinse with cool water using gentle circular motions.
  7. Follow with moisturizer and sunscreen if going outside.

Tip: Turmeric can temporarily stain skin, so be careful with clothing. Use 1-2 times per week. The stain will fade within a few hours.

Cucumber & Aloe Eye Pads

⏱ 10 minutes ⭐ Very Easy 👁️ For Puffy & Tired Eyes

Ingredients:

  • 1 fresh cucumber
  • 2 tablespoons aloe vera gel
  • 1 tablespoon rose water
  • Cotton pads or round slices

Instructions:

  1. Chill a cucumber in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.
  2. Cut thin slices or blend cucumber to extract juice.
  3. Mix cucumber juice with aloe vera gel and rose water.
  4. Soak cotton pads in the mixture or use cucumber slices directly.
  5. Lie down and place pads/slices over closed eyes.
  6. Relax for 10-15 minutes.
  7. Remove and gently pat the area dry.

Tip: This is perfect for reducing puffiness and dark circles. Use daily for best results, especially in the morning or after a long day.
