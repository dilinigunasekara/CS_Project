DIY Beauty Recipes

Create natural beauty products at home with simple, effective recipes

🌿 Turmeric, Honey & Aloe Vera Face Scrub

⏱ 15 minutes ⭐ Easy 💧 For Dry & Sensitive Skin

🧴Ingredients:

  • Turmeric powder – ½ teaspoon
  • Honey (pure or organic) – 1 tablespoon
  • Aloe vera gel (fresh or natural) – 1 tablespoon
  • Optional (for mild scrubbing): Rice flour or oatmeal powder – ½ teaspoon

🧪Instructions:

  1. Take a clean bowl and add honey and aloe vera gel.
  2. Add turmeric powder and mix well to form a smooth paste.
  3. (Optional) Add rice flour or oatmeal powder for gentle exfoliation.
  4. Wash your face with lukewarm water and pat dry.
  5. Apply the scrub evenly on your face, avoiding the eye area.
  6. Gently massage in circular motions for 1–2 minutes.
  7. Leave it on for another 5 minutes.
  8. Rinse off with lukewarm water and pat dry.
  9. Apply a light moisturizer if needed.
    💡Tip: Tips & Precautions
  • Use this scrub 1–2 times per week only.
  • Always do a patch test before first use.
  • Use a small amount of turmeric to avoid yellow staining.
  • Best suitable for normal to dry and acne-prone skin.
  • Avoid using on broken or irritated skin.
  • Use fresh aloe vera gel for best results.
  • Apply at night to avoid sun sensitivity.

Coffee Body Scrub

⏱ 10 minutes ⭐ Easy ☕ For All Skin Types

🧴Ingredients:

  • 1 cup ground coffee (used or fresh)
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup coconut oil (melted)
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Combine ground coffee and brown sugar in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add melted coconut oil and mix until well combined.
  3. Stir in vanilla extract and cinnamon if using.
  4. Store in an airtight container at room temperature.
  5. In the shower, apply the scrub to wet skin in circular motions.
  6. Focus on areas like elbows, knees, and feet for extra exfoliation.
  7. Rinse thoroughly with warm water.
    💡Tip:
  • Coffee grounds help reduce cellulite appearance and improve circulation.
  • Use 2-3 times per week for smooth, glowing skin.

🌾 Rice Flour Face Scrub

⏱ 5 minutes ⭐ Very Easy 🌿 For Oily & Acne-Prone Skin

🧴Ingredients:

  • Rice flour – 1 tablespoon
  • Honey – 1 tablespoon
  • Aloe vera gel or milk – 1 tablespoon
  • Optional:
  • Rose water – 1v teaspoon
  • Turmeric powder – a pinch (for glow)

🧪Instructions:

  1. Take a clean bowl and add rice flour.
  2. Add honey and aloe vera gel (or milk).
  3. Mix well to form a smooth paste.
  4. Clean your face with lukewarm water.
  5. Apply the scrub evenly on your face.
  6. Gently massage in circular motions for 1– 2 minutes.
  7. Leave it on for 5 minutes.
  8. Rinse off with lukewarm water.
  9. Pat dry and apply moisturizer.
    💡Tip:
  • Use once or twice a week only.
  • Use finely ground rice flour to avoid skin damage.
  • Always do a patch test before use.
  • Suitable for oily, normal, and acne-prone skin.
  • Avoid rubbing too hard on the skin.
  • Do not use daily, as it may cause dryness.
  • Best applied in the evening.

Turmeric & Yogurt Brightening Mask

⏱ 15 minutes ⭐ Easy ✨ For Dull & Uneven Skin Tone

🧴Ingredients:

  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 tablespoons plain yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon gram flour (optional)

🧪Instructions:

  1. Mix turmeric powder with yogurt in a small bowl.
  2. Add honey and lemon juice, stirring until smooth.
  3. Add gram flour if using for extra exfoliation.
  4. Apply evenly to clean face and neck, avoiding the eye area.
  5. Leave on for 15-20 minutes until it dries slightly.
  6. Rinse with cool water using gentle circular motions.
  7. Follow with moisturizer and sunscreen if going outside.
    💡Tip:
  • Turmeric can temporarily stain skin, so be careful with clothing.
  • Use 1-2 times per week. The stain will fade within a few hours.

Cucumber & Aloe Eye Pads

⏱ 10 minutes ⭐ Very Easy 👁️ For Puffy & Tired Eyes

🧴Ingredients:

  • 1 fresh cucumber
  • 2 tablespoons aloe vera gel
  • 1 tablespoon rose water
  • Cotton pads or round slices

🧪Instructions:

  1. Chill a cucumber in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.
  2. Cut thin slices or blend cucumber to extract juice.
  3. Mix cucumber juice with aloe vera gel and rose water.
  4. Soak cotton pads in the mixture or use cucumber slices directly.
  5. Lie down and place pads/slices over closed eyes.
  6. Relax for 10-15 minutes.
  7. Remove and gently pat the area dry.
    💡Tip:
  • This is perfect for reducing puffiness and dark circles.
  • Use daily for best results, especially in the morning or after a long day.

🌰 Chickpea Flour Face Scrub

⏱ 10 minutes ⭐ Very Easy ✨For Dull Skin

🧴Ingredients:

  • chickpea flour / Besan – 1 tablespoon
  • Honey – 1 teaspoon
  • Yogurt or milk – 1 tablespoon
    • Optional:
  • Turmeric powder – a pinch (for glow)
  • Lemon juice – a few drops (for oily skin)

🧪Instructions:

  1. Take a clean bowl and add chickpea flour.
  2. Add honey and yogurt (or milk) to form a smooth paste.
  3. Add a pinch of turmeric or a few drops of lemon juice.
  4. Wash your face with lukewarm water and pat dry.
  5. Apply the scrub evenly to your face.
  6. Massage gently in circular motions for 1–2 minutes.
  7. Leave it on for 5–10 minutes.
  8. Rinse with lukewarm water and pat dry.
  9. Apply moisturizer if needed.
    💡Tip:
  • Use 1–2 times per week.
  • Do a patch test before using for the first time.
  • Gentle massage only; avoid rubbing too hard.
  • Suitable for all skin types, especially oily or acne-prone skin.
  • Avoid using on broken or irritated skin.
  • Yogurt or milk helps hydrate and soothe the skin

Avocado Hair Mask

⏱ 20 minutes ⭐ Easy 💚 For Dry & Damaged Hair

🧴Ingredients:

  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 tablespoons coconut oil
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 egg yolk (optional, for extra protein)

🧪Instructions:

  1. Mash the avocado in a bowl until smooth and creamy.
  2. Add coconut oil, olive oil, and honey, mixing well.
  3. If using, add the egg yolk and mix until fully incorporated.
  4. Apply the mask to damp hair, starting from the roots to the ends.
  5. Cover your hair with a shower cap or warm towel.
  6. Leave on for 30-45 minutes for deep conditioning.
  7. Rinse thoroughly with cool water and shampoo as usual.
    💡Tip:
  • This mask is perfect for restoring moisture to dry, damaged hair.
  • Use once a week for best results.

🌿 Fenugreek & Aloe Vera Hair Scrub

⏱ 20 minutes ⭐ Easy 💚 For Hair Fall

🧴Ingredients:

  • Fenugreek powder – 2 tablespoons
  • Fresh aloe vera gel – 2 tablespoons
  • Coconut oil – 1 tablespoon

🧪Instructions:

  1. Mix fenugreek powder and aloe vera gel well.
  2. Add coconut oil and make a smooth paste.
  3. Apply to the scalp and hair roots.
  4. Gently massage for 3 minutes.
  5. Leave for 20 minutes and rinse with shampoo.
    💡Tip:
  • Helps reduce dandruff and itching.
  • Aloe vera soothes the scalp.
  • Use lukewarm water to rinse.

🍃 Curry Leaves Hair Scrub

⏱ 20 minutes ⭐ Easy 💚 For Dry & Short Hair

🧴Ingredients:

  • Fresh curry leaves – 15–20 leaves
  • Coconut oil – 2 tablespoons
  • Aloe vera gel – 1 tablespoon
    • Optional:
  • A few drops of essential oil (rosemary or lavender)

🧪Instructions:

  1. Wash the curry leaves thoroughly and crush them to a fine paste using a blender or mortar and pestle.
  2. Mix the crushed leaves with coconut oil and aloe vera gel.
  3. Add a few drops of essential oil if desired and mix well.
  4. Section your hair and apply the scrub to your scalp evenly.
  5. Gently massage the scalp in circular motions for 3–5 minutes.
  6. Leave it on for 15–20 minutes.
  7. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water and shampoo as usual.
    💡Tip:
  • Use once a week for best results.
  • Do a patch test before the first use.
  • Best for hair fall reduction, promoting hair growth, and controlling dandruff.
  • Avoid using on broken scalp or open wounds.
  • Massage gently; avoid excessive force to prevent hair damage.
  • Use fresh curry leaves for maximum benefits.

🌿Fenugreek Hair Scrub

⏱ 20 minutes ⭐ Easy 💚 For Dry and Damaged Hair

🧴Ingredients:

  • Fenugreek seeds – 2 tablespoons
  • Coconut oil – 2 tablespoons
  • Warm water – as needed

🧪Instructions:

  1. Soak fenugreek seeds in water overnight.
  2. Grind the soaked seeds into a slightly coarse paste.
  3. Add coconut oil and mix well.
  4. Apply the scrub gently to the scalp.
  5. Massage in circular motions for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Leave it for 15–20 minutes.
  7. Wash off with mild shampoo.
    💡Tip:
  • Do not scrub harshly to avoid scalp irritation.
  • Use once a week for best results.
