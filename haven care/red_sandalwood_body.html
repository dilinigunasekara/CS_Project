Red Sandalwood Body Scrub for Radiant Skin

Red sandalwood (Rakta Chandan) is a natural ingredient widely used in traditional beauty treatments. It helps improve skin tone, remove dead skin cells, and give the skin a healthy glow.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons Red sandalwood powder
  • 2 tablespoons Gram flour
  • 1 tablespoon Honey
  • 2–3 tablespoons Milk or Rose water

How to Make

  1. Take red sandalwood powder and gram flour in a bowl.
  2. Add honey and milk (or rose water).
  3. Mix well until it forms a thick scrub paste.

Main Ingredient Benefits

A body scrub made with red sandalwood is a natural way to achieve radiant and healthy skin. Red sandalwood helps gently remove dead skin cells and impurities from the skin surface. When mixed with gram flour, honey, and milk, it exfoliates the skin while also providing moisture and nourishment. This natural scrub helps improve skin tone, reduce tanning and dullness, and make the skin smoother and softer. Regular use can help maintain bright, glowing, and naturally radiant skin.
